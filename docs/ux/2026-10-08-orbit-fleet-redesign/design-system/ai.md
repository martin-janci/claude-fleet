# AI and Jev

Two engines suggest; neither acts. A rule comes first, then Jev, then an LLM.

- **Jev** answers closed-set questions (a choice, yes or no, a score) with a pinned model, per-organisation consent, redaction, a daily budget and a breaker. Modes are off, shadow and assist. There is no auto mode.
- **The LLM** drafts text (summaries, PR descriptions, mission briefs) through `claude -p` on a host of the same organisation.

## How a suggestion looks

- Jev: the ✦ pill **Proposed by Jev**, then the reason, then one undo link: "Proposed by Jev · mercury: same repo, 2 idle slots · Change".
- LLM: the ✎ pill **Drafted**, then the source, then **Regenerate** and **Clear**.
- A field Jev filled keeps the `ai-pre` accent ring until the person touches it.
- Nothing shows while Jev is deciding: no loader, no placeholder.

## Where AI never decides

Trust, roles and shares; a task's organisation; mission completion and Verified checks; kill or clean-up readiness; choosing a host or account by the numbers; priority or size; the order of Needs you. Jev never pre-selects Approve on a push or a permission.
