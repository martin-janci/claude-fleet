# QuestionCard

The card a waiting session shows in its conversation: what it asks, the exact command or text, and numbered answers.

**The consumer provides** the question as a headline, its age, the command or detail in mono, and the answer options in the agent's own order, each with its number key.

**Do**
- Keep the agent's option order; 1, 2 and 3 answer from the keyboard.
- Always offer "Answer in your own words…".

**Don't**
- Never let Jev or an LLM pre-select or highlight an answer on a push or a permission.
- Don't hide the command behind a toggle; the person approves what they can read.
