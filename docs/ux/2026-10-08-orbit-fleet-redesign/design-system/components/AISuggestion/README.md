# AISuggestion

How a machine suggestion looks: a ✦ "Proposed by Jev" pill with its reason and an undo, or a ✎ "Drafted" pill with its source, Regenerate and Clear.

**The consumer provides** the engine (Jev for closed-set decisions, the LLM for drafted text), a one-line reason or source, and the undo link ("Change", "Not waiting").

**Do**
- Fill a field Jev proposed with the `ai-pre` accent ring until the person touches it.
- Show nothing, not a loader, while Jev is deciding; the proposal appears when ready or not at all.
- Run in shadow mode first, then assist. Never auto.

**Don't**
- Never pre-select Approve on a push or a permission.
- Never use AI for trust, roles, shares, a task's org, mission completion or Verified checks, kill or clean-up readiness, choosing a host or account by numbers, priority or size, or the order of Needs you.
