Rules for every form
· Check on blur and on submit, never on each key.· A disabled verb always says why under it.· Server errors go in a banner at the top of the body; the person's input is kept.· Secrets are write-only: they show where they are stored, never their value, and never reach an agent.· Jev and AI only propose: the outline and the chip say so, the person confirms.· No full-screen overlay. No spinner while waiting on a person; say what we wait for.· A form with more than one decision becomes steps; each step can go Back.· Every wizard is one fleet.form/1 spec, drawn as a dialog here or as a ChatForm in a chat.· Closing a changed form asks once: "Discard changes?" inline in the footer.
